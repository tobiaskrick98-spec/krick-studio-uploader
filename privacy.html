Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-24  ·  Last updated: 2026-05-24

This Privacy Policy describes how the Krick Studio Uploader application ("the App", "we") handles information when used to publish content to a YouTube channel.

Who runs the App

The App is operated by Tobias Krick, an individual content creator and professional athlete based in Germany. Contact: tobiaskrickyt@gmail.com.

What data the App accesses

When you authorize the App with your Google account, the App receives an OAuth 2.0 token that grants the following scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — permission to upload video files to your own YouTube channel.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube — permission to set video metadata (title, description, tags, category, privacy status, made-for-kids flag) on uploads created by the App.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly — permission to read the channel's upload history so the App's daily-cap logic can verify whether a video has already been published that day.

How the App uses that access

The App uses these scopes only to:

  • Upload short-form video files that the creator has explicitly placed in the local upload queue.
  • Attach the metadata the creator has prepared in advance (title, description, tags, category).
  • Read the channel's most recent uploads to enforce a "max one upload per calendar day" cap.

The App does not read your private videos beyond what is needed for the daily-cap check, does not modify videos that were not uploaded by the App, and does not delete any videos.

Where data is stored

All data handled by the App is stored locally on a single private server controlled by the creator:

  • Your OAuth refresh token and access token are stored in a file named token.json on that server only.
  • The local upload queue (mp4 files + metadata json) is stored on that server only.
  • Upload logs are stored on that server only.

No data is transmitted to any third-party server, analytics service, advertising network, or shared backend. The App is single-user: only the creator's own Google account is authorized.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any data with third parties.
  • We do not use the data for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not collect analytics, telemetry, or usage statistics from third-party users (because there are no third-party users).
  • We do not train any AI or machine-learning model on the data.
  • We do not transfer data outside Germany / the EU.

How to revoke access

You can revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time by visiting myaccount.google.com/permissions, finding "Krick Studio Uploader" (or its OAuth client name), and clicking "Remove Access". After revocation, the App will be unable to upload further videos until it is re-authorized.

Data deletion

Because the App stores tokens and logs only on a single private server, data deletion is performed by deleting token.json and the local log files from that server. There is no central database to delete from.

To request manual deletion of any data, contact tobiaskrickyt@gmail.com.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly process data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the change. Material changes will be posted to this page.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: tobiaskrickyt@gmail.com.
